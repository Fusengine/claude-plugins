## Top gaps to act on (ordered by cost avoided)

1. Always-loaded volume (rows 1-4): about 42 KB and 356 lines against a 200-line target, paid again by every subagent and teammate; imports do not reduce it.
2. Emphasis density (rows 5-7): 35 uppercase emphasis tokens and many "never" lines that the docs say recent models over-apply or read as absolute.
3. Delegation cost (rows 9-11): trio on every code touch and "split by default" against 3-5 agents, 15x token multiplier, coding less parallel; no deterministic cap set.
4. Verification stacking (row 12): eLicit + Verify + sniper + challenger + executor challenger; Opus 5 and Sonnet 5.5 pages advise against model-initiated extra review, but endorse an independent reviewer.
5. Check-ins and status volume (rows 14-16): milestone reports multiply lead input; add the carry-through sentence for Sonnet-medium agents.
6. Prompt-only hard rules (row 8) and missing permission guidance (row 22).
7. Mandatory `sequentialthinking` before every multi-step action (row 19): no Anthropic source for it.
8. Per-tool-result injection (row 17): documented misread risk, owner-decided scope unclear; test, do not change.
